Data notes

EPA per play
Expected points added. Each play is scored by how much it changed the points a team should expect from that down, distance and field position. Above zero is good for an offense, below zero is good for a defense.
Success rate
Share of plays with positive EPA.
Net yards per pass play
Passing yards minus sack yards, divided by attempts plus sacks.
Explosive play rate
Share of plays that are a pass of 20+ yards or a run of 10+ yards.
Red-zone touchdown rate
Touchdowns divided by drives that reached the opponent's 20.
Simple Rating (SRS)
Average point margin adjusted for the opponents played; zero is a league-average team. It follows the idea behind Pro Football Reference's SRS and can differ from their published figure by up to about a point.
